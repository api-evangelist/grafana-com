You can use Grafana Assistant Investigations to automatically discover incidents and help find root causes—and this AI-powered Grafana Cloud feature recently got a major upgrade to give you even more confidence in its findings. You can read more about the behind-the-scenes effort in our new engineering blog Unprompted , where we get into harness engineering, context compaction, benchmarking, and keeping agents alive and working well in long-running sessions. In this post, we'll focus on how you can get the most out of this product iteration in conjunction with our other features.
