When you're the on-call engineer and something breaks, you can quickly find yourself deep in a series of tools you don't regularly use—switching tabs, copying query results, and manually stitching together a picture of what's happening and why. People are increasingly turning to AI to get around this, but the results can be a mixed bag. For example, many of today's observability, data warehousing, or project management tools come equipped with powerful AI assistants that can help you get answers quicker.
